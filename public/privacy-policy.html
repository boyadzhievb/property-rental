Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Introduction

Property Rental is a personal property management application designed to help property owners manage their short-term rental operations. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all data entered into this application. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles, how it is stored, and your rights regarding that data.

This application runs entirely on your device. All data is stored locally and never leaves your machine. There are no external API calls, no cloud services, no analytics, and no telemetry.

1. What Data the App Handles

The following data is entered by you and stored exclusively on your device:

  • Guest information: name, phone number, email address, and notes
  • Room information: room names, pricing, capacity, and status
  • Reservation details: check-in and check-out dates, guest count, pricing, and status
  • Payment records: amounts, payment method (cash, card, or transfer), and notes
  • Task records: titles, categories, and completion status
  • App preferences: theme selection (light/dark/system) and language preference
  • Property settings: property name

2. How Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using the browser's IndexedDB storage. App preferences (theme and language) are stored in localStorage.

No data is transmitted to any external server, cloud service, or third party. The application functions entirely offline after initial installation.

3. No Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, transfer, or disclose any data to third parties. Since all data remains on your device, there is no mechanism for data to leave your control.

There are no advertising networks, social media integrations, or third-party SDKs embedded in this application.

4. No Analytics or Tracking

This application does not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, advertising identifiers, or tracking technologies. We do not collect usage statistics, device information, IP addresses, or any form of telemetry.

Your usage of the app is completely private.

5. No Cookies

This application does not use cookies or any similar tracking technologies. Local storage is used solely to persist your app preferences (theme and language selection) on your device.

6. Data Export and Backup

You may export all your data as a JSON backup file using the Export Backup feature in Settings. This file is saved directly to your device. You are solely responsible for the security of exported backup files.

Importing a backup replaces existing data on the device.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data remains on your device until you choose to delete it. You can erase all data at any time using one of the following methods:

  • Use the Reset Data option in the app's Settings (a backup is automatically downloaded before reset)
  • Clear your browser's site data
  • Uninstall the application

8. Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times:

  • Access and Export: Download a complete copy of your data at any time via Settings > Export Backup
  • Delete: Erase all data via Settings > Reset Data, or uninstall the application
  • Portability: The exported JSON file can be imported into another instance of the app on a different device
  • Offline access: All features work without an internet connection

9. Children's Privacy

This application is a business tool intended for property owners and managers. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since no data is collected or transmitted externally, there is no risk of unauthorized collection of children's personal information.

10. International Data Transfers

This application does not transfer any data internationally. All data remains on your local device and is never transmitted to any server, regardless of location.

11. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (device lock, encryption, etc.). Since data never leaves your device, there is no risk of server-side data breaches. We recommend keeping your device secured with a passcode or biometric lock.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
